# Playbook, Instagram Content Calendar

→ [[Marketing]]

How a month of posts is planned, written, approved and scheduled, for our own channels and for clients.

## Monthly rhythm

| Week | Work |
|---|---|
| 1 | Themes for next month, agreed with the client |
| 2 | Copy and design for every post |
| 3 | Client approval round, one round only |
| 4 | Scheduling and a final check of every link |

## Rules

- Every post has one job: teach, show, or ask. Never all three.
- Captions are written for the person scrolling, not for the algorithm.
- A post that mentions a customer needs that customer's written consent first.
