# Decision, Monthly Retainers Only

→ [[Product]]

Northwind Studio sells monthly retainers with a defined scope, and no longer quotes one-off projects without a retainer behind them.

## Context

One-off projects took as long to sell as a retainer and ended just as the client started to see results. Reporting, the thing clients value most, only works over months.

## Decision

Every engagement is a monthly retainer with a written scope from the [[Service Catalog]]. A website build is sold as the first phase of a retainer.

## Consequences

- [[SOP, Client Onboarding]] starts every engagement the same way.
- Proposals reference the catalog instead of a custom quote.
